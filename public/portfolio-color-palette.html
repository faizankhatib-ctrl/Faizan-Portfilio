The palette

Six colors: two dark blues for structure, slate for quiet supporting text, amber reserved for the moments that need attention, and two neutrals to keep everything readable.

#0B2545

Deep navy

Primary color, header, nav

#13315C

Midnight blue

Section backgrounds, cards

#4F6D9A

Slate blue

Borders, icons, muted text

#E9B44C

Golden amber

Buttons, links, highlights

#F5F7FA

Cloud white

Page background

#1B1B29

Ink

Body text on light sections

Applying it

A rough 60/30/10 split works well: navy and midnight carry most of the weight, cloud white and ink handle readable content, and amber shows up only where you want the eye to land.
